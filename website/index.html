Governance

Token utility is separate from corporate ownership.

No Equity

NNX does not itself represent shares of Nationalux Lab Inc.

No Corporate Vote

Token holders do not receive director-election or corporate voting rights merely by holding NNX.

No Guaranteed Return

NNX does not promise appreciation, dividends, profits or a guaranteed market.

No Fixed Redemption

NNX is not presented as a fiat-backed stablecoin and has no promised 1:1 fiat redemption.

Legal & Risk

Compliance-by-design

Nationalux intends to separate token utility, corporate ownership, data rights and regulated financial activities. Any public distribution, fundraising, exchange, redemption, custody or client-transfer functionality should be reviewed under the laws applicable to the relevant activity and jurisdiction.
