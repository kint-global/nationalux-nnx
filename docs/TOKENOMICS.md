## Circulation and release policy
- Target initial circulating supply at exchange listing: **1,199,988 NNX (approx. 12%)**.
- Existing non-company holdings of **71 NNX** are included in that target.
- Up to **1,199,917 NNX** would therefore need to move from company-controlled holdings into qualifying circulation to reach the target.
- The target does **not** represent a commitment to sell the entire amount into the market.
- NNX does not currently use a predetermined time-based unlock schedule. Treasury releases are tied to actual ecosystem activity, partnerships, AI/developer participation and liquidity needs.
- Material treasury allocations or material increases in circulating supply will be disclosed in advance.

## Supply policy
The smart contract retains mint functionality. **9,999,900 NNX is the current total supply, not a hard maximum supply.** No additional issuance is currently scheduled. Existing treasury holdings are intended to be used before additional issuance is considered. Any material future issuance is subject to formal Nationalux Lab Inc. approval and prior public disclosure.
